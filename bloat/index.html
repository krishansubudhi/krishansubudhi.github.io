Each PR is tagged feature, fix, test, docs, refactor, chore or other from its title prefix, labels or wording. The grade compares agents with humans within each type that has at least 3 agent and 3 human PRs (marked ✓): median net lines, median deletions and net lines per file, weighted by agent PRs. A is 0.8x the human growth or lower, B up to 1.1x, C up to 1.5x, D above. It does not count how often a PR grows code, because feature PRs normally do.
